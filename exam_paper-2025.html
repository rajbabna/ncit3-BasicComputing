Title: Air India Flight AI-171 Crash – June 2025

On 12 June 2025, Air India Flight AI-171, a Boeing 787-8 Dreamliner, crashed shortly after takeoff from Ahmedabad Airport, en route to London Gatwick. The aircraft, carrying 242 people (230 passengers and 12 crew), tragically crashed into the hostel block of B.J. Medical College in the Meghaninagar area of Ahmedabad just 36 seconds after take-off.

The crash resulted in the deaths of 241 people on board and at least 39 individuals on the ground, making it one of the deadliest aviation disasters in India's history. The sole survivor was a British national. The aircraft reportedly experienced a loss of power and thrust, and investigators are examining whether the emergency power system was activated at the time of the crash.

The incident has had wide-ranging repercussions, affecting Air India's reputation and operations, as well as prompting an urgent review of aviation safety protocols.
